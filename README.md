# 👋 Moin, I'm Sören

I'm a **Product Engineer** based in Hamburg, Germany.

I build apps, tooling, and open-source projects you can explore here on GitHub.

## 🧠 My Philosophy

I believe great software is defined by **clarity, reliability and impact**, not by frameworks or trends.

As a Product Engineer, I think in **systems**, not features.

- 🔍 I care about understanding the problem before writing code.
- 📈 I connect technical decisions to product outcomes.
- ⚙️ I optimize for robustness and simplicity over cleverness.
- 🏗️ I design for long-term maintainability, not only short-term velocity.
- 👥 I see engineering as a leadership discipline, not just implementation.

I build software people can rely on — technically solid, thoughtfully designed, and built to evolve.
